Radar

Which hashtag channels are in use around the bot. Every group message the radio hears starts with a channel byte and a short check code; the radar compares those with the keys of the channels on your radio (private ones too, added there are picked up within a minute), about 200 known channel names, the names on the ChannelRadar card and tens of thousands of generated variants (mesh-zl, maastricht-chat, ...; shown from 2 messages). A channel is only listed when both match, so it is certain. Messages are never decrypted and nothing is sent. A message heard via several repeaters counts once. Refreshes every minute.

Add a channel to the radar
A hashtag channel needs only its name. A channel with its own key (private, or shared by QR code) needs the key too; it is only counted, never decrypted. Works at once, also back through the packet log; nothing is put on your radio (that is the Add to radio button in the list).

Unknown channels

Messages on channels whose name is not in the list: private channels that are not on your radio, or a hashtag name nobody added yet. Shown by channel byte (several channels can share one). Know or suspect the name? Click , type it, and the radar checks it against the logged messages of that byte; a match is recognised from then on (and counted back through the log).

Channels you added (on the ChannelRadar card or with Add a channel on the Channel radar tab) on which the radio has not heard anything in the last 30 days. The radar keeps looking for them; as soon as a message comes by, the channel moves to the Channel radar list. Channels from far away (another region) usually stay here.

ChannelAdded via

Every repeater the bot heard, from its own records (nothing is sent). Direct = the bot's radio hears it without a hop in between (neighbour discovery on the BotNetwork card). Bots = how many bots of the bot network hear it directly. Same prefix = other repeaters whose key starts with the same byte and that are also within 50 km of the bot (a path with 1-byte hops cannot tell those apart). Click a tile to filter, click a repeater for all details. Refreshes every 5 minutes.
