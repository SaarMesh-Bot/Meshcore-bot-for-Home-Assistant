Radar

Which hashtag channels are in use around the bot. Every group message the radio hears starts with a channel byte and a short check code; the radar compares those with the keys of the channels on your radio (private ones too, added there are picked up within a minute), about 200 known channel names, the names on the ChannelRadar card and tens of thousands of generated variants (mesh-zl, maastricht-chat, ...; shown from 2 messages). A channel is only listed when both match, so it is certain. Messages are never decrypted and nothing is sent. A message heard via several repeaters counts once. Refreshes every minute.
